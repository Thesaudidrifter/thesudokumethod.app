Is every puzzle fair?
Yes. Every puzzle has one solution and never requires a guess. The daily Challenge is the same for every player, and there are no virtual coins or boosters to buy.
Does it need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-in, and it works offline. Your games and settings stay on your phone.
Which phones does it run on?
Android 8.0 or newer. On a foldable, the board and tools adapt when you unfold your phone.
