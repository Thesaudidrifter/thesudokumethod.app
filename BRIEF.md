# The Sudoku Method: site brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("build the rest as you think fit"), 7 November 2026.
Workflow and scroll engine from scroll-craft; performance, accessibility and scrollytelling rules from
claudedesignskills (modern-web-design). The awesome-design-skills styles were read and not adopted, because the
app already has its own colours and type. Facts come from the app.

## The job
- What: the home of The Sudoku Method, a logic-only Sudoku app for Android.
- For: people who already like Sudoku and want to get better at it.
- Must believe: there is always a next move, and this app teaches you to find it.
- Next action: get it on Google Play (until launch: "Coming soon to Google Play").
- Also: host the privacy policy for Play Console, and later app-ads.txt for AdMob.
- Later: a short ad. The board is a pure function of one number, so the same scenes can be rendered to video.

## Grammar: worked solve (new)
One real puzzle is solved once, from the first digit to the last, on one board that never leaves the page.
- Sequence: solve order. Nothing appears out of order, and nothing is invented: every step is the app's grader.
- Nav: the game's own top bar, with the live timer and mistake count.
- Hero: the board at rest in 3D, with the claim beside it.
- Close: an actual input. The visitor places the last digit, and the result is computed from their own session.
- Bans: photographs, device mockups, feature cards, statistics, testimonials, section numbers, scroll cues.

## Signature move
Lights out at the stall. The X-Wing turns the lights back on from the centre of its rectangle.

## Feeling curve
1. Curiosity: a board at rest, digits lifted off the paper.
2. Recognition: the camera moves in on one box, and the reasoning is shown before the digit.
3. Flow: digits fall quickly, each with a real technique name.
4. Tension: the singles run out, pencil marks fill the board, the room goes dark.
5. Aha (peak of the scroll): the X-Wing, lights on, and the board falls.
6. Breadth: the rest of the app, real screens only.
7. Play: one board in eight themes.
8. Stillness, then agency (the end): one cell left, and it is the visitor's.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you scroll through a real Sudoku being solved, then place the last digit yourself."

## Device score
| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | pinned 3D planes, pointer tilt | The opening has to be physical, and the board is the only image |
| Hint | camera move, sweep lines | One box at a time is how the hint really works |
| Flow | scroll-scrubbed digit run | The hand drives the solve |
| Stall | ground drift to Onyx | The only slow colour change on the page |
| X-Wing | iris from the rectangle | Used once: a change of state |
| App | pan rail | Sideways reads as breadth |
| Themes | hard cuts | Each notch is a new theme |
| Close | input | The page stops moving and starts responding |
